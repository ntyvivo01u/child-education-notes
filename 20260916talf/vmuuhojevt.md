# 深圳恒生医院IHCU急救技能培训活动走进艾维国际幼托

> 更新时间：2026-09-16 (UTC+8)

2023年2月28日， 深圳恒生医院走进艾维国际幼托成功开展了双语CPR急救技能培训活动。

On Feb 28th 2023, a first-aid skill training course was successfully carried out at Ivy International Nursery & Kindergarten, organized by Shenzhen Hengsheng Hospital IHCU.

深圳恒生医院运营管理部总监兼健康体检科主任王爱好副主任医师用幽默风趣的语言，为艾维国际幼托教师讲解心肺复苏知识。

Doctor Wang of Shenzhen Hengsheng Hospital gave a lecture and demonstrated CPR skills in an interesting way to the teachers of Ivy International Nursery & Kindergarten.

在实操环节中，两位学员积极参与互动，结合生活实际案例模拟场景，展现对伤者实施救援的操作过程。王爱好副主任医师在旁细致指导、评估、调整、规范学员实施动作。

In the practice session, two teachers were invited to participate in the interaction session, to show how to react in emergency situation and how to perform the basic CPR. At the same time, President Wang was on site to evaluate their performance and guide them.

心肺复苏对日常生活中心脏骤停的抢救有非常重要的作用，如果能抓住这黄金4分钟，能及时抢救到很多需要帮助的人。

CPR plays a very important role in the rescue of cardiac arrest patients in our daily lives if encountered such, and if the first golden 4 minutes can be seized, many people in need can be rescued in time.

老师们纷纷为恒生IHCU（国际医疗服务中心）这场活动点赞，认为活动实用性强，以后应对学生日常突发情况救助更有信心了。

Teachers have expressed that this activity is very practical. They not only realize the important role of CPR in daily life to rescue cardiac arrest, but also have more confidence to react to the emergency at school.

校长Chris 也表示，这次的课程让大家受益匪浅，希望与恒生IHCU建立更紧密联系，未来共同组织以健康为主题的活动。

Principal Chris was impressed by our training program. He expected that IHCU organize more health-oriented activities such as how to dress a wound and how to fixate bone fractures in the future.

深圳恒生医院IHCU旨在为全球家庭客户提供一站式，以患者为中心的国际健康管理服务平台，为外籍患者创造无语言障碍的就医体验。

Shenzhen Hengsheng Hospital IHCU aims to provide a one-stop, high-end patient-orientated health management platform for families and create a comfortable hospital experience without language barriers for foreign patients.

为了满足健康需求，深圳恒生医院IHCU团队可为**国际学校、外企定制健康培训 、健康趣味活动及知识讲座。 **

To meet public health needs, Shenzhen IHCU team can customize medical activities and health knowledge seminars for international schools and foreign companies.
